The video was very interesting; I normally watch Veritasium in my free time, 
and I hadn’t seen the video before, so it was a pleasure to watch. 
I learned how rotary phones worked, how electrical signals work with phones, 
and the logistics of what it takes to make a phone call. I was surprised 
to see how easily someone can intercept your calls/messages if they 
really wanted to. I think security is treated as an afterthought, because
creators might favor just getting the job done and not consider security 
vulnerabilities in the process of designing an already complex system.
